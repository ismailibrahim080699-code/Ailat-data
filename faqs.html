FAQs

Common questions about your account and services

How do I access my account?

Sign in using your registered email and password. Your email must be verified before protected account pages can be opened. Keep your password, OTP and transaction PIN private.

Which services are available?

Buy Data and Buy Airtime are connected to the provider integration. Exam PIN plans currently include WAEC, NECO and NABTEB. A service appearing on the page does not by itself prove that every live transaction has been tested successfully. Electricity and Cable TV live purchases are not yet enabled; do not make a payment for them.

What should I do if an order is pending or fails?

Check Transaction History first. If the status is pending, processing or unclear, do not submit the same order again until its status has been checked. Contact Support and include the transaction reference. Never send your password, OTP or transaction PIN.

Where can I see my order records?

Open Transaction History from your dashboard or account menu. It displays transaction records returned by the connected backend. Where an exam PIN has been issued, the available PIN and serial details appear with the transaction.

How do my Transaction PIN and fingerprint work?

Use the security options available in Settings to manage your transaction authorization. Purchases may ask you to authorize with your Transaction PIN or registered fingerprint/passkey. Never share your PIN or approve an action you did not start.

How do I fund my wallet?

Open Fund Wallet and follow the payment instructions. Wallet credit is made only after the payment provider's transaction is verified. If payment succeeds but your balance is not updated, do not pay again; contact Support with the payment reference. Live end-to-end payment testing may still be pending.

Can I withdraw money?

You can submit a withdrawal request with your bank details. Requests are reviewed by an administrator; approval deducts the requested amount from your wallet. The bank transfer is handled manually, so approval does not mean the transfer has already been completed. The request is marked paid only after the transfer is confirmed.

How do referrals work?

Open your Referral page to copy and share your referral code or link. Any reward depends on the eligibility and commission rules configured for your account. Do not assume a referral has earned a reward until it appears in your account records.

How do I contact Support?

Open Support to submit a ticket and review replies or ticket status. Include the transaction reference and a clear description of the issue. Do not include passwords, OTPs, transaction PINs, recovery codes or full card/bank credentials.
