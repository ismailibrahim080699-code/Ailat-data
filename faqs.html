FAQs

Common questions about your account

How do I access my account?

Sign in using your registered email and password. Your email must be verified before protected account pages can be opened.

Why is a service not completing an order?

Some services are awaiting verified provider/API configuration. A form preview does not mean an order has been sent or completed.

Where can I see my order records?

Open Transaction History from your dashboard/account menu. Only records actually returned by the connected backend can be displayed.

Where are my security settings?

Open Settings, then Security. Transaction PIN and biometric enrollment must remain unavailable until a secure, verified process is implemented.

How do referrals work?

Use your referral page to copy your referral code or link. Rewards depend on the eligibility and commission rules actually configured for the account.

Can I add a bank account or withdraw now?

Do not enter bank details until a verified, secure payout flow is available. The page will state clearly when that functionality is ready.
